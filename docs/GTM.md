# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. Target User
**The Profile:** CTO or "Head of Engineering" at a Seed to Series A startup (10–50 employees).
**The Pain:** They have a bloated AI stack (Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT Team, Claude Team) because they allow "departmental shadow spend" where every engineer picks their own IDE/LLM. They are starting to get "subscription fatigue" from the board and need a quick win to show fiscal responsibility without slowing down development.

## 2. Trigger Moments (The "Search/Scroll" Intent)
Right before wanting this tool, they are:
- Scrolling through their **Ramp or Mercury dashboard** wondering why "OpenAI" appears 12 times in one month.
- Googling **"Cursor vs GitHub Copilot vs Claude Code"** to decide which one to standardize on.
- Googling **"cancel ChatGPT Enterprise contract"** because they over-forecasted seats.
- Checking a **"SaaS cost reduction checklist"** on a Notion template.

## 3. Where they hang out
- **X (Twitter) Lists:** "AI Engineering" or "Founder/CTO" lists (e.g., lists curated by Gergely Orosz or Guillermo Rauch).
- **Subreddits:** `r/SaaS`, `r/CTO`, and `r/LocalLLaMA` (for the API spenders).
- **Slack/Discord:** "Lennny's Newsletter" Slack, "Warp" Discord, and "Y-Combinator" Bookface (if accessible).
- **Newsletters:** "SaaS Weekly" or "Pragmatic Engineer" comment sections.

## 4. First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)
1. **The "Shadow Audit" DM:** Cold DM founders on X who recently tweeted about their "AI Stack." Instead of selling, ask: *"Hey, we built a tool to see if you're double-paying for Cursor + Copilot. Want a 60-second audit link? No signup."*
2. **The "Marketplace Bounty":** Find companies on LinkedIn hiring for "AI Engineers" and DM the CTO: *"Hey, noticed you're scaling. If you have unused AWS/OpenAI credits from a previous pivot, we can help you liquidate them via Credex to fund your new hires."*
3. **Engaging with "Switching" Threads:** Monitor keywords like "Switching to Cursor" on X. Reply with a comparison screenshot from the audit engine: *"Cursor is great, but don't forget to cancel those 15 Copilot seats. Here's a tool to find the rest of the leaks."*
4. **IndieHackers/Show HN:** Post a "Data-Only" thread: *"We audited 50 startups' AI spend and found 30% overlap. Here's the tool we used."*

## 5. The Unfair Distribution Channel
**Credex’s Secondary Market Data.** 
Only Credex knows who is *currently* trying to sell unused credits. We can reach out to these sellers and offer them the Audit as a "Valuation Tool." If they want to sell their Claude credits, we audit their current usage to tell them *exactly* how many seats they can safely offload without hurting their team. This turns "sellers" into long-term Credex customers.

## 6. Week-1 Traction
- **250 Audits Completed.** (Viral sharing via shared results URLs).
- **15 "High Savings" Leads.** (Audits detecting >$1,000/mo wastage).
- **3 Credit Resale Inquiries.** (Users clicking through to Credex.rocks after seeing their "waste").
- **Conversion Rate:** 6% from "Landing Page" to "Lead Capture" (Email submitted post-audit).
