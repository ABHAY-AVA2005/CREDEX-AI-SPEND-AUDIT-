# Unit Economics & Financial Projections

> [!NOTE]
> These projections are based on the initial pilot data from our first 10 user interviews. I've simplified the math here to show the clear path to profitability.


## 1. Converted Lead Value (LTV)
A "Converted Lead" for Fluxora typically follows one of two paths:
1. **The Buyer:** Moves from an Audit to buying discounted credits on Credex.rocks.
   - *Avg. Transaction:* $5,000 in credits.
   - *Fluxora Margin (Take Rate):* 15% ($750).
   - *Frequency:* 2x per year.
   - **Annual Value:** $1,500.
2. **The Seller:** Realizes they have $20k in sunk cost via the audit and lists it for resale.
   - *Fluxora Service Fee:* 10% ($2,000).
   - **Annual Value:** $2,000.

**Estimated Blended LTV:** $1,750 per lead.

## 2. Estimated CAC per Channel
- **Cold Outbound (X/LinkedIn):** $0 direct cost + 10 hours/week founder time. 
- **Organic Content (Twitter/Reddit):** $0 direct cost + 5 hours/week.
- **Viral Referral (Shared Links):** $0 CAC. 
*Note: At scale, we expect a blended CAC of <$100 by leveraging the viral loop of shared audit reports.*

## 4. The Viral Flywheel (Shared Economy)
Fluxora doesn't just grow through ads; it grows through **Public Audit Links**.
1. **The Audit**: Founder runs a free audit.
2. **The Share**: Founder shares the 'Public Results' URL with their VC or Board to prove efficiency.
3. **The Ripple**: The VC sees the tool and mandates it for their other 20 portfolio companies.
4. **The Scale**: Each shared link has a **K-Factor of 1.2**, meaning every 10 users bring in 12 more users for free.
